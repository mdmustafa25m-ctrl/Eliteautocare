# Elite Auto Detailer

Create a premium, modern, luxury car detailing and washing website for a business called ELITE AUTO CARE in Hyderabad, India.



DESIGN STYLE



- Premium luxury automotive aesthetic inspired by high-end detailing brands.

- Black and dark charcoal backgrounds with white text and elegant gold accents.

- Large, high-quality images of luxury cars, detailing, ceramic coating and professional car washing.

- Clean layout, smooth scrolling, subtle animations and elegant typography.

- The website must look like a professional agency designed it, not a basic template.

- Fully responsive and optimised for Android phones, tablets and desktops.



WEBSITE SECTIONS



1. Navigation bar



- Logo text: ELITE AUTO CARE

- Menu: Home, Services, Gallery, About, Contact

- Prominent “Book a Service” button.



2. Hero section



- Use a stunning full-width photograph of a luxury car being professionally detailed.

- Heading: “EXCELLENCE IN EVERY DETAIL.”

- Subheading: “Premium Car Wash & Detailing for Those Who Expect More.”

- Buttons: “Explore Services” and “Book on WhatsApp”.



3. Services

   Create premium service cards for:



- Premium Car Wash

- Interior Deep Cleaning

- Exterior Detailing

- Ceramic Coating

- Paint Protection

- Full Car Detailing



Each card should have a relevant image, short description and a “Enquire Now” button. Use editable example prices or display “Contact for Pricing” until the business confirms its actual prices.



4. Why Choose Us



- Professional care

- Attention to detail

- Quality products

- Convenient booking

  Do not invent awards, certifications or customer statistics.



5. Gallery



- Display a stylish grid of car washing, polishing, detailing and before-and-after photos.

- Use high-quality automotive imagery with consistent styling.



6. About Us



- Heading: “More Than a Wash. A Complete Car Care Experience.”

- Add a short, professional introduction that the business owner can customise.



7. Contact and location



- Business name: ELITE AUTO CARE

- Location: Hyderabad, Telangana

- Phone: [ADD BUSINESS PHONE NUMBER]

- WhatsApp: [ADD WHATSAPP NUMBER]

- Google Maps: [ADD GOOGLE MAPS LINK]

- Opening hours: [ADD BUSINESS HOURS]

- Add a contact form and a direct WhatsApp enquiry button.



8. Footer



- Logo, quick navigation links, contact information and social media links.



FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS



- All navigation links should work.

- WhatsApp buttons should open a chat with the configured business number.

- Enquiry buttons should open WhatsApp with a prefilled message mentioning the selected service.

- Contact form must validate required fields and show a clear confirmation; don't pretend a message was sent unless a real submission backend is configured.

- Add smooth scrolling and mobile-friendly navigation.

- Optimise image sizes and page loading speed.

- Use accessible text contrast and readable font sizes.



IMPORTANT



- Build the complete working website, not just a mockup.

- Use reusable components and clean code.

- Use placeholder business details that are easy to replace.

- Do not invent real reviews, customer testimonials, awards, certifications or business claims.

- Make the final result visually impressive, trustworthy and suitable for a premium automotive business.
